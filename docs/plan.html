Bistrița Alpină online shop

Platform recommendation, the legal model for selling firearms, payments, the photo-to-listing workflow, palettes and the launch checklist. Facts carry their source; anything I could not verify is marked UNVERIFIED.

The answer: should we use Shopify?

Yes: Shopify Grow, with firearms, ammunition and air guns sold as "reserve online, pay and collect in store". Settle card payments before building.

Why Shopify. It covers the owner's main wish best: add products from the phone with the built-in camera and barcode scanner, remove backgrounds in the mobile media editor, and get AI descriptions from Sidekick. Its policy no longer has a firearms clause. armesimunitie.ro, a Romanian gun shop, already runs on Shopify with exactly this model: weapons and ammunition are pickup only, and payment is by bank transfer or cash on delivery.

The catch is card payments. Shopify Payments excludes "firearms, holsters, ammunition, or weapons". NETOPIA's eligibility page lists "articole și produse de vânătoare (arme albe și arme de foc…)" among the fields it does not accept, which could affect the whole store. PayU Romania's position is UNVERIFIED. EuPlătesc's list of refused fields does not name weapons, but it has no Shopify app.

Decision rule. Before building, ask EuPlătesc, PayU Romania, NETOPIA and Banca Transilvania in writing whether they will take this store: a hunting store whose firearms and ammunition are reserved online and paid in the shop.

  • If PayU or NETOPIA says yes, use Shopify Grow.
  • If only EuPlătesc or BT says yes and online card payment is essential, use Gomag Advanced (€37/month + VAT). It has EuPlătesc, e-Factura and the couriers built in. The design moves over as-is.
  • If nobody says yes, Shopify still works. Launch with cash on delivery, bank transfer and payment in the shop, like armesimunitie.ro.

Not WooCommerce. It carries no platform risk, but a non-technical owner would have to maintain plugins, security and backups.

How selling firearms online works in Romania

The site follows the model Romanian gun shops already use, and the EU rule (Directive 2021/555, Art. 8) that a licensed dealer checks identity and authorisation before or at delivery.

Product classHow it sells on the siteWhy
Firearms (cat. B), essential parts, barrelsReserve · collectBuyer needs a police autorizație de procurare (valid 90 days, extendable once). The armurier writes the sale onto it. The buyer registers the gun with the police within 3 working days (Legea 295/2004 art. 16, 22, 106). All five couriers ban firearms.
All ammunitionReserve · collectSold only for guns listed on the buyer's permit, up to the quantity on the permit, at most 300 per weapon per calibre (art. 42). Couriers ban it.
Air guns ≤ 220 m/s (cat. C23)Reserve · collectStill need an authorisation (art. 57). Cargus also bans airsoft.

Optics, clothing, boots, accessoriesShipNormal checkout, courier or locker.
Night-vision sights, laser sights, suppressorsDon't listBanned for private persons (art. 5(2)). Handheld thermal spotters are not sights. Confirm with the lawyer.

The legal text I read is the 2014 republication of Legea 295/2004. Later amendments exist (OG 38/2024, approved in 2025), and I did not obtain the implementing norms. Art. 17(5) leaves distance sales to those norms, so a lawyer or IPJ Bistrița-Năsăud must confirm the flow before launch.

Stock upload: photo to live listing

The owner's whole job is one photo plus a price. The prototype's owner dashboard (Panou proprietar → Adaugă produs) shows the exact screens.

  1. Snap 1–3 photos and scan the barcodePhone, any background. The Shopify app has a product camera and barcode scanner.
  2. Background removed, studio background and shadow addedPhotoroom API: $0.02/image (removal), $0.10/image (AI background + shadow). Shopify's media editor can also remove backgrounds on mobile, free for now.
  3. Product identifiedEAN lookup (EAN-Search.org: 5,000 lookups for €19/month) plus Claude reading the model name off the photo.
  4. Title, Romanian and English description, category and specs writtenClaude API, structured output. Uncertain specs are flagged "verifică" for the owner.
  5. Owner checks price and stock, then taps PublishSale mode (ship / 18+ / reserve) is set by category, so a rifle can never be added to the courier checkout by mistake.

Cost per product (3 photos, Sonnet 5.5): 1×$0.10 + 2×$0.02 = $0.14 for images, plus about $0.026 for Claude, plus €0.004 for EAN. That is about $0.17 + €0.004. Token counts are estimates. For 1,000 products: about $170 plus the €19 EAN plan.

How to build it. Start with Shopify's built-ins (camera, background removal, Sidekick) for zero extra cost. If Sidekick's Romanian is weak during the trial, add a small custom "Adaugă produs" app: a phone web page calling Photoroom and Claude, then creating the product through Shopify's Admin API. It is one focused piece of work, and the prototype's screen is its spec.

Avoid fully generative image editing (Gemini or OpenAI image models) for packshots: it can change engravings or reticles. Use it only for lifestyle scenes.

Orders and daily operations

  • Courier orders: pack, then generate the AWB in Expedly or Fluxsales (FAN, Sameday, Cargus, lockers). The customer gets tracking automatically.
  • Invoices: SmartBill (Shopify app from $17.99/month) or Oblio (official app, free) issue the invoice. Whether the Shopify-to-SmartBill route sends invoices to ANAF e-Factura automatically is UNVERIFIED. Test it in the trial; B2C e-Factura has been mandatory since 1 Jan 2025.
  • Reservations: statuses go "Documente de verificat" → "Gata de ridicare" → "Ridicată". On Shopify this needs a reservation form with document upload, either an app or custom theme code. The app choice is UNVERIFIED; I'll pick it during the build.
  • In-store POS: Shopify POS card payments likely depend on Shopify Payments, so keep the shop's existing card terminal and use POS only for stock sync (UNVERIFIED).

The prototype

A working storefront and owner dashboard in Romanian and English, as one file: dist/bistrita-alpina-prototip.html in the repository. It opens in any browser, offline too. It contains:

  • Shop: categories with hunting-specific filters, search with suggestions, product pages with spec tables, and the split cart (courier items and reservations side by side).
  • Checkout: guest checkout, a document step for reservations, courier / easybox / pickup, card / cash on delivery / bank transfer, the 18+ confirmation, consents and the withdrawal form.
  • Owner dashboard ("Panou proprietar", via the person icon): today's tasks, the photo → AI → publish flow (the AI is simulated in the prototype), orders with document checks and AWB, and stock with +/− buttons and online visibility.

Four QA agents tested it: functional, visual and accessibility, copy and compliance, and a final automated run. That run passed 3,134 checks at phone and desktop sizes in both languages, including with browser storage blocked. Untested: real iPhone Safari and screen readers. Products, prices, orders and specs are demo data; several specs are unverified against manufacturer pages.

What I took from guns.com, and what I didn't

  • Kept: filters per category (calibre, action, barrel, magnification…), the key specs on every card, a clear spec table, a trust strip, calibre shortcuts, and a "certified used" idea for second-hand guns brokered in the shop.
  • Added for Romania: a split cart (courier items and reservations side by side), the document step in checkout, price per cartridge for ammunition, an estimated delivery date, the Omnibus "lowest price in 30 days" on discounts, guest checkout first, and RO county and company-invoice fields.
  • Dropped: the FFL transfer, financing, auctions, Make-an-Offer and US state compliance. No fake reviews: product pages say "no verified reviews yet" until real ones exist.

Marketing limits

Google Merchant Center (Shopping and free listings) does not allow guns, ammunition, scopes and sights, or fighting knives. Meta ads prohibit firearms, ammunition and telescopic sights, and Meta commerce prohibits weapon sales. Exclude those product types from the shopping feeds. Advertise clothing, boots, binoculars and hunting gear, with 18+ targeting on Meta. Whether binoculars and handheld thermals count as "optics" for Meta is UNVERIFIED; test with a small budget.

Launch checklist

From the owner

  • Company name, CUI, Reg. Com., address, phone, email, WhatsApp, opening hours
  • Armurier authorisation number (shown in the footer)
  • Written answers from EuPlătesc, PayU, NETOPIA and BT on accepting the store
  • Courier contract (FAN and/or Sameday) and the real shipping fees and free-shipping threshold (the prototype uses 24,99 / 14,99 / 500 lei as placeholders)
  • Choice of palette; original logo files if available (the SVG logo was redrawn from photos of the sign)
  • Confirm the shop policies the site states: same-day call-back, 14-day reservation hold, 1–3 day delivery, 18+ for knives

Legal (lawyer)

  • Confirm the reservation and pickup flow against the current consolidated Legea 295/2004 and its norms
  • Terms and conditions, privacy policy (permit data, retention for the dealer register), cookie policy
  • Withdrawal button (OUG 18/2026, in force 19 Jun 2026) and 14-day withdrawal. Confirm that in-store pickup contracts aren't distance contracts
  • ANPC SAL pictogram linking to reclamatiisal.anpc.ro; no EU ODR/SOL link (that platform closed 20 Jul 2025)
  • Withdrawal flow format: whether OUG 18/2026 needs a two-step "withdraw → confirm" with a dated acknowledgement UNVERIFIED
  • Cash limit for in-store payment of expensive firearms (believed to be 10,000 lei/day under Legea 70/2015) UNVERIFIED
  • Retention and secure storage of uploaded permits and authorisations

Build

  • Shopify trial: test Sidekick in Romanian, SmartBill e-Factura, Expedly labels
  • Turn the prototype into a custom Shopify theme (same design, filters, split cart, reservation form)
  • Load the real catalogue: photograph the stock with the workflow above
  • Domain, SSL, Google Business Profile, analytics with consent
